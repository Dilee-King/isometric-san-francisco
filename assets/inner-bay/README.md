# Inner Bay landmark artwork

The eleven daylight plates and the Jack London Square and Sausalito wet-night edits use native 1536 × 1024 framing and the fixed city camera. Each PNG is paired with its exact imagegen prompt. Regenerate structural references from the retained citywide source with `python tools/expanded_render.py references`. The Wharf and city-core plates guided the illustration style. Source depth and terminal geometry supply the placement of moving traffic.

`tools/expanded_tiles.py` feathers the plates over the source-rendered city. At coastal plates, the blend extends into the near shore to retain beach foam, dock texture and crane booms. Four appearances use the same registered image grid. The wider water field is continuous across the region boundaries.
