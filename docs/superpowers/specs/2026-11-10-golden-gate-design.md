# Golden Gate waterfront extension

The next release adds the San Francisco shoreline from Fisherman's Wharf through Aquatic Park, Fort Mason, Marina Green and the Presidio to the Golden Gate Bridge. It shares the published waterfront's pan/zoom surface, fixed camera and native detail scale. The Bay Area overview keeps its regional purpose and marks the expanded detail. The interface remains English.

Extend the existing registered artwork and source atlas. A separate inset would interrupt the waterfront pan; rebuilding the east would unnecessarily replace reviewed scenes. Use bounded open geometry and elevation, native-size art for the new neighborhoods, and a continuous bridge drawing tied to its deck geometry. Keep the existing eastern artwork, location jumps, vehicle projection and lighting/weather controls. Bridge and park traffic follows roads; vessels stay clear of land and bridge supports. Weather and transport remain illustrative.

The existing open-water rectangles are a compositing defect. Blend offshore pixels into one appearance-specific water field using distance from registered shore, piers, and built features. Preserve near-shore wet reflections and land detail, including where the existing artwork only approximately matches the source. Apply the same operation in all four day/night and dry/wet appearances.

Acceptance: a real browser can pan through the entire extension, see both towers of the Golden Gate Bridge, inspect the new area at the existing native detail scale, switch day/night and weather, and move to the regional atlas without resetting the clock. Compare the same wide water view against v0.3. Show actual captures and a recording before pushing; then confirm the public release on GitHub Pages.
