# San Francisco and the Inner Bay

Extend the published 2D atlas through three cumulative releases. v0.6 covers the San Francisco mainland and its nearby islands, both Bay Bridge spans and their road connections through Yerba Buena Island. v0.7 adds the Port of Oakland, Jack London Square and the Alameda ferry waterfront. v0.8 adds Fort Baker, the shore road and Sausalito, with ferry service back to Ferry Building.

The existing camera and world coordinates register a geographic citywide illustration and selected native-scale landmark plates. The published v0.5 artwork remains the detailed base of its corridor. One sparse Deep Zoom grid requests native tiles over illustrated land, islands and crossings, with a shared low-zoom field over the bay. Reuse the current weather, time, vehicle renderer and region cache. The expanded masks share one packed texture. Releases retain independent scene descriptors over the common extension assets.
