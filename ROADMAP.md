## 1 — Calibrated illustration sample

- [ ] Produce at least four contiguous, reviewed full-detail quadrants around the Ferry Building.

## 2 — Navigable map

- [ ] Build a React/TypeScript/Vite/OpenSeadragon viewer and DZI export flow.
- [ ] Add compact controls, credits, coverage state and 390 px layout.
- [ ] Verify pan/zoom and world-to-image registration in a real browser.

## 3 — Transport

- [ ] Cars: directions, lanes, turning, waits and occlusion.
- [ ] Boats: water routes, docking dwell and clipped wakes.
- [ ] Aircraft: occasional registered overflights and navigation lights.
- [ ] Independent toggles, simulation worker, global pause and reduced motion.

## 4 — Environment

- [ ] Registered day, twilight and night appearance with local light masks.
- [ ] Clear, cloudy, coastal fog and rain, independent of hour.
- [ ] Freeze weather/transport/time together and verify zoom registration.
- [ ] Record authored appearance versus any genuinely relightable shadows.

## 5 — Expand and release

- [ ] Expand contiguous city/waterfront coverage without lowering the sample quality.
- [ ] Add meaningful code checks, versioned manifests and a measured performance budget.
- [ ] Choose asset storage/hosting and publish a verified map release with update instructions.

Wider Bay Area coverage, airport ground operations, live feeds and LCZ research functions have no delivery date in this initial roadmap.
